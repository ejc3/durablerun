# Postmortem: PR5.3c, stuck, stats, sizes and schema version 11 (its review and the narrow re-review of the fold) (PR #117)

PR5.3c gives an operator three commands over one queue, `stuck`, `stats` and `sizes`, on five new reads of `OperatorReads`, with the index of schema version 11. One review of the branch's head found no HIGH finding, one MEDIUM and seven LOW. The MEDIUM is a product defect: a run that is due and that the claim's own requirement refuses was in no leg of `stuck` at any grace, so `stuck --fail-if-any` exited 0 for a run that no tick will ever take, while `stats` counted the run as due and `explain` called its task stuck. Six of the eight count by the repository's rule. The verdict: every one of the six was found by outside review, the defect that matters was a consequence the plan had written down as intended, and the check that was built to hold the finder to the engine could not see it, because it asked the question from one side only. This document's own audit then found the same gap in the sweep's two legs, and a second part of the fold closed it and replaced the reads the first part had added with bounded ones.

A narrow re-review of the fold alone then found no HIGH, no MEDIUM and nine LOW, every one of them made by the fold, and six of the nine count: 12 in all. Two are product defects of the three new legs. `unexamined` was true on any backlog deeper than the limit and one, whatever the grace, so the word a monitor would read said nothing. And `atLeast` was false with more refused rows in the window than the leg listed. A last fold fixed the six, and no review follows it.

**This document is adversarial toward the MACHINERY and blameless toward
people.** Never "who wrote it", "should have noticed", "was careless" — those

## Severity

Nothing shipped: the pull request is a draft, and the review ran before any merge. What would have shipped without it, worst first.

A command whose name is `stuck`, with a flag made for a monitor, that stays silent for the one kind of run nothing will ever move (finding 1). The legs held what the engine's next claim or sweep would take. A due run that the claim refuses is taken by neither, so it was in no leg, at a grace of zero or of an hour. The review's state was one task whose stored retry strategy is not JSON, an hour after it came due: a claim took nothing, a sweep took nothing, `stuck --grace 0s --fail-if-any` listed nothing and exited 0, `stats` printed `pendingRunsDue` 1 with a claim lag of the hour, and `explain` printed `pending-due-unclaimed` with the verdict `stuck`. An operator who watched the queue through `stuck --fail-if-any` would have seen green for as long as the row stood. Only `--older-than` found it, by its age. No engine path writes such a row, and a row no engine path writes is what an operator's command is for. The engine does write one run a claim refuses, the due run of a task past its cancellation deadline, and that task was listed, as a task the sweep cancels.

A list that skipped its first row (finding 3). `eventWaiters` read 1,001 waits in the order of run and step, core sorted those by task and cut to 1,000. With more than 1,001 waits on one event, the wait that the read's order put last could be first in the order the list printed, and it was left out, while DESIGN.md promised the first 1,000 in the order of task, run and step. `explain` prints that list. It takes an event with more than a thousand waits to see it.

A gate that excused more than it said (finding 6). The measured surface under the libSQL plan reader holds the reader to a measurement: a statement that grows beside a backlog must be refused by the reader, or excused. On main the excuse was a due range that drives another step. This branch made it any due range the plan read at all, and its BUILD.md entry and its `gate-changes:` line both called the change a tightening. A variation of a shipped statement that grew while it read a lone range on any of four columns, through any index and with or without a LIMIT, passed both of the oracle's properties. No such variation exists on the branch, so nothing wrong passed. What was lost was the oracle's power to catch the reader passing one.

Three statements that said more than the code. DESIGN.md said a live task whose enqueue instant is outside its bounds is listed however old was asked. It is reported only when its leg reads it, and a task enqueued past its bound is the last row of its state, so for such a row what `stuck --older-than` exits with depends on `--limit`: 0 for a limit of one, 10 for two and for five (finding 2). The comment on what `stats` prints said a queue whose every run waits on an event nobody emits is `quiet`, and it is `active` (finding 7). The doc of `nextWakeAtMs` said it is the earliest instant of any row a gauge counts, and the enqueue instant of a live task is not among them (finding 8).

What the fold itself would have shipped, which the re-review found. A flag no monitor could use (finding 10): `unexamined` was true whenever a row of a window was not settled or the window came back full, whatever the grace. Twenty-two healthy pending runs due for one second, under the default grace and limit, printed `unexamined: true` with nothing listed, and a claim then took all 22. A word that said less than its doc (finding 9): with one run a claim takes beside 2,000 it refuses, an hour on, `dueNotAdmitted` printed 20 rows with `atLeast: false`, where the doc of `atLeast` says true when the database holds more rows of the kind than the list. A hold that could not fail for a window of deadlines left out of the merge (finding 16): on MySQL, whose windows of deadlines are three, a running or a sleeping task no sweep cancels would have gone unlisted with every gate green. And three statements that said more than the code or the tests: that every read of the batch is bounded by its LIMIT (finding 14), that a case holds `explain`'s answer for a lapsed lease no sweep reclaims (finding 15), and that `cancelOverdue` lists the task of a due run no claim admits once the task is past its deadline (finding 17).

## Recurrence

A corrupt row read as a clean one recurred, in finding 1, and it has now appeared in every round that built an operator's read. PR5.3b1's round met it as an event with no instant read as an ordinary event and as a rogue state printed hidden, and built a generated NULL case for every persisted integer of four tables and one definition of what `inspect` cannot read. PR5.3b2's round met it as a rogue state that `explain` named nothing for, and built one function that `inspect`'s exit and `explain`'s cause both read. Both mechanisms work on a value a read selects: core decodes it, and what the decoder refuses is named in `corrupt`. That is what they check. What they were supposed to hold is that no operator's read passes over a row the engine holds unsafe without saying so. A command that lists rows by the engine's own predicate does not select the value that makes the row unsafe. The predicate reads it inside the database and the row is not answered at all, so there is nothing for a decoder to refuse and no entry to generate a case for. The two earlier mechanisms are a decoder's, and a WHERE clause is not a decoder. This round's legs read no predicate of refusal at all. They read the rows a move is owed to by their instant alone and compare them with what the engine's own statement answers, so a row that any predicate of the engine drops is listed, for the three kinds of row a move is owed to.

## Fix-induced defects

Six of the twelve: findings 9, 10, 14, 15, 16 and 17, every counted finding of the second round. All six were made by the fold's second part, which replaced the negated reads with windows and added the sweep's two legs. The windows brought the two words whose meaning findings 9 and 10 are about, and the three windows of deadlines on MySQL that finding 16's hold did not reach. The same part wrote the sentence of finding 14 beside the measurement that replaced the old one, the sentence of finding 15 about the case it had just written, and the doc of finding 17 in the commit that added the leg it left out. The three findings that count 0 are the fold's as well. The fold was read as new code by the narrow re-review, which is how these were found. The last fold is not read again. ## Root cause

The legs were specified by where their rows come from, the engine's own predicates, and every check that was built asked whether a leg's rows are the engine's. None asked whether the engine's predicates cover the rows an operator needs, because the specification had already answered that: a leg means what the engine means. So the differential, the seeded answers, the instants and the walks all compared a leg with the statement it was built from, and they agreed, as two readings of one predicate do. The rows that matter most to the command's name are the rows the engine will not touch, and a check that starts from what the engine takes cannot arrive at them. The gauges were checked from the other side, against a count of a dump, and they were right about the run the legs left out. The two checks stood in one case and nothing compared their answers. The first part of the fold compared them for the runs that are due and stopped there, at the instance the review had named. This document's audit of that fix asked the same of the leases and the deadlines, and the second part compares all three.

The other counted findings share a smaller cause. A case, a sentence or a line was true of the instance in front of its author: ids that happened to sort alike, a value planted on the side of a bound where the sentence holds, a queue as it was before a later commit added a gauge, a change read as "reports more" where it meant "excuses more". Nothing in the machinery asks of a case what input would make it fail, or of a sentence what later change would make it false.

The second round's cause is the first's, turned on the fold. The windows were built to answer one question, how to find a refused row without reading the backlog, and every case and sentence written for them answered that question: a row behind its window, a row reached once the rows ahead are taken, a read bounded by its LIMIT. The two words a leg prints beside its rows were given the meanings they would have if that question were the only one. `atLeast` kept the doc of a leg read to its end. `unexamined` was true whenever anything at all was left, because at a grace of zero, where every case ran, anything left is something the leg could list. The sentence about cost was measured beside the rows of the cases, which a claim admits. Each was true of the states in front of its author and of no other, which is what the paragraph above says of a case, a sentence and a line.
